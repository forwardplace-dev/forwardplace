IRONOTE

Available ・ Android

IRONOTE is a workout log for weights and reps. Like a paper notebook, you write your exercises, weights and reps on one sheet a day.

Get it on Google Play
WHAT IT DOES
  • The dotted box already shows your previous set or last session's numbers. If they're the same, double-tap to log it.
  • This week's strip on the home screen shows which days you trained and what you did.
  • You can check weight, volume and estimated 1RM for each exercise as a chart and as numbers.
  • You can build programs based on a percentage of your max. Each time you log, the program moves to the next session.
  • If you allow notifications, the rest timer keeps running on the lock screen after you close the app.

YOUR DATA

Everything is stored on your device, and we never receive it. IRONOTE doesn't connect to the internet and has no ads. Save your records to a file to move them to a new phone, or export a CSV for spreadsheets. Weights are in kg in this version.
