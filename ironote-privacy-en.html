IRONOTE Privacy Policy

Provided by: Forward Place
Effective date: November 9, 2026

Forward Place ("we") sets out below how the smartphone app "IRONOTE" (the "App") handles user information.

1. Our approach

Everything is stored on your device, and we never receive it. We also never receive your device backup (see 7) or files you save yourself (see 5). The App does not send your information to our servers or collect it.

We run no servers for the App, and the App contains no tools for showing ads, analyzing usage, or collecting crash reports. The App does not connect to the internet.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored on your device (in the App's internal storage). We never receive a copy. (If device backup is on, Android may include it in the backup of your own Google account. See 7.)

  • Exercises (the names and body parts of exercises you add): used in sheets, programs, and when you choose exercises.
  • Workout records (dates, sheet titles, the order of exercises, and for each set the weight, reps, time logged, note, and failed mark, plus a one-line note per exercise): used to show your sheets, pre-fill last time's numbers, and show the daily summary.
  • Programs (the name, exercise, max weight, percentages, reps, number of sets, progress, and other details you enter): used to calculate and show target weights and reps.
  • Rest timer settings and status (the target time, the time it started, and so on): used to keep the timer accurate even when you close or lock the screen.
  • Settings (display options, which home sections are open, which exercises appear in Progress, when to show the next update notice, and so on)
  • A copy of your records from before a restore (up to two, on your device)

3. Permissions the App uses and why

  • Notifications: to show the rest timer and its buttons (pause, stop, and so on) in notifications and on the lock screen. You can still use the timer in the App without allowing this.
  • Vibration: to let you know, silently, that your rest target time has been reached.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): to vibrate exactly at your target time even while the screen is off. If you don't allow this, the alert may be a little late.

The App does not use location, contacts, photos, the camera, the microphone, or network access.

4. Sending information outside and sharing with third parties

The App does not send your information to our servers or to third-party servers, and does not share it with third parties.

To let you know when a new version is available, the App uses Google Play's in-app update feature. The Google Play Store app on your device checks for and downloads the new version. When it does, the Play Store communicates with Google, and Google's privacy policy applies. We never receive this information.

5. Backup and export

Only when you choose to do so in Settings, the App saves your records as a backup file or a CSV file to a place you choose (on your device or in a service such as Google Drive). You are responsible for managing these files, and we never receive their contents. If you save them to a cloud service, that service's terms apply. The files are not encrypted, so please be careful when you give them to or share them with others.

6. Keeping information safe

Information on your device is kept in the storage area Android assigns to the App, and is read and written only as needed for the App to work. We have no way to retrieve this information. Information you send us in an inquiry is handled only as needed to reply and keep a record. Please take care of your device itself (for example, by setting a screen lock).

7. Retention and deletion

Information on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App's data in your device settings. We hold no user information, so there is no need to ask us to delete anything. If device backup is on, Android may include the App's data in the backup of your own Google account. This is done by Google, and we cannot see its contents. Backup and CSV files you saved from Settings are not deleted when you remove the App. Delete them from where you saved them when you no longer need them.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Minors should use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.

10. Contact

If you contact us, we use the name, email address, and message in your email only to reply and keep a record, and we do not share them with third parties. We manage this information appropriately. Inquiry emails are kept in our email service (Google's Gmail) and deleted about one year after we finish handling them. You can also contact us below to request disclosure, correction, or suspension of use of information we hold, or to make a complaint. We will promptly provide our name, address, and other details required by law upon request.
