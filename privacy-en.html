Anti-Dopa Privacy Policy

Provided by: Forward Place
Effective date: October 18, 2026

Forward Place ("we", "us") provides the Android app "Anti-Dopa" (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Our basic approach

The App does not send your information to our servers or collect it. We do not run our own servers, and the App contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting tools.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored only on your device:

  • The apps and websites you choose to limit
  • Your settings (wait time, penalty mode, extension settings, usage-time notification on/off and time, etc.)
  • Text you enter in the App (reasons for opening an app, your personal vow, etc.)
  • A record of when the wait screen was shown for each app
  • The status of your free trial and subscription
  • For each app and site you limit, how many times you held back (chose “Don't use it” on the wait screen or left while waiting) and an estimate of the time you didn't spend (the time you usually spend per open of that app, calculated from usage data). This is shown as your “Self-control log” on Home. It stays if you remove an app or site from your list, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Daily records are deleted automatically after 30 days.

3. Permissions and why we use them

Permissions you grant

  • Accessibility service: Used to detect when you open an app or website you have chosen to limit, and to show the wait screen. The name of the app in the foreground and the URL shown in your browser's address bar are checked on your device only, to compare them with your list. They are not stored or sent anywhere.
    The App does not use the accessibility service to read the content of web pages or other apps, read what you type (including passwords), take screenshots, or record your screen. To take you away from an app or website you chose to limit, the App may go back, go to the home screen, close a browser tab opened inside another app, or open a blank page in your browser (or, if a blank page is not available, a search engine's home page). It may also show the wait screen and related buttons over the screen.
  • Usage access: Used to read and display how long you have used your apps, to create usage-time notifications (if turned on), and to estimate the time you didn't spend when you hold back on the wait screen. The usage time itself is not stored by the App or sent anywhere.
  • Battery optimization exemption: Used so the App keeps working reliably in the background.
  • Notifications: If you turn on "usage-time notifications", the App uses this to tell you, at a time you choose each day, your usage this week compared with the same point last week. The notification content is created on your device and is not sent anywhere. All other features work without this permission.

Permissions granted automatically at installation

  • Viewing the list of installed apps (so you can choose which apps to limit)
  • Vibration
  • Detecting device restart (to keep scheduling usage-time notifications after a restart)
  • Google Play Billing (for subscriptions)
  • Network access (used only by Google Play Billing for purchases and by Google Play In-App Updates to check for app updates; both communicate only with Google Play)

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties.

  • Purchases and restoring your subscription are handled by Google Play. Your payment information is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your card details.
  • "Report a problem" only opens your email app. You decide what to send. The draft email includes the App version, device model, and Android version to help us investigate; you can delete them before sending.
  • The share feature only passes text and a link to your device's share screen.

5. Security

Apart from communication with Google Play described in section 3, the App does not send information off your device. Information is stored in the storage area Android assigns to the App, and we cannot access it. Please protect your device with a screen lock.

6. Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes the information stored on your device. Information may be included in your device backup; this is handled according to Google's terms. We cannot access your backups.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access or delete your personal information.

Because the App does not collect or store your personal information on our side, we do not hold App data that could be accessed, corrected, or deleted. You can delete everything at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

If you contact us by email, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your email and our record of it (see section 10).

If the UK or EU GDPR applies to you: we process the emails you send us because we have a legitimate interest in answering your questions and keeping a record of support requests. We will respond to a request about your rights within one month. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Minors should use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.

10. Contact

If you contact us, we use your name, email address, and message only to reply and keep a record. Emails are stored with our email provider, Google (Gmail), which may process them outside your country, including in the United States. We do not share them with anyone else, and we delete such emails about one year after we finish responding.

For questions, requests, or complaints: forwardplace.support@gmail.com

11. Language

This policy is also available in Japanese. If you use the App in English, the English version applies.
