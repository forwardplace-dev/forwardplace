FAQ

How is this calculated?
Daily time × 365 days ÷ 24 hours gives the number of days per year.
Is what I enter sent anywhere?
No. Everything is calculated on this page only.
Where can I find my screen time?
On Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls. On iPhone: Settings → Screen Time.
How are the remaining years worked out?
Your expected lifespan minus your age. The lifespan starts at 80 and you can change it. It is a rough estimate, not a prediction.
